I am Zhangyu Huang, a Ph.D. researcher in Artificial Intelligence at Macau University of Science and Technology.

My research explores how autonomous agents can predict multiple plausible futures, identify consequential risks, and make reliable decisions with uncertainty-aware learning.

01 / RESEARCH DIRECTION

From uncertain predictions
to informed actions.

My work connects probabilistic prediction with downstream decision making. Rather than treating uncertainty as an isolated prediction metric, I study how uncertain futures should influence what an agent attends to and how it acts.
