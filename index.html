Ph.D. Researcher · Artificial Intelligence

Zhangyu Huang

Macau University of Science and Technology

I study decision making under uncertainty for autonomous driving. My research connects multimodal trajectory prediction with reinforcement learning to help autonomous agents make safe and reliable decisions.

I am interested in how agents can reason about multiple possible futures and identify the information that matters for their next action.
